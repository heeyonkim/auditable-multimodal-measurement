## Submitted figure captions

**Figure 1. Proposed measurement architecture.** Multimodal raw data is processed using a suite of available tools to extract information. The proposed architecture aligns their outputs by person, time, and channel and links them to the source. Researchers specify how to combine observations into measures for analysis. Extraction, measurement, and interpretation remain separate.

**Figure 2. Audio surfaces departures that transcripts miss.** Each point represents an answer compared with that executive’s own baseline. Highlighted answers were relatively typical in transcript language but unusual in vocal delivery or interaction timing.

**Figure 3. Behavioral patterns vary across channels.** Rows represent interviews, labeled by the CEO interviewed, and columns represent channels. Color shows how far the interviewer departed from her pattern in other eligible interviews. There were seven comparison interviews for words, turn-taking, voice, and facial movement while speaking, and five for facial movement while listening. Hatched cells indicate insufficient observable evidence to calculate a score.
